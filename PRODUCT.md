# Product

## Register

product

## Users

RawSift serves photographers working on Windows 10 or Windows 11 who need to review large shoots quickly while safely importing selected originals. They may connect a Sony camera directly, insert a card reader, or open a local source folder. Their attention belongs on photographs, not on file-management mechanics or decorative interface chrome.

## Product Purpose

RawSift combines culling and verified ingest in one focused desktop workflow. It lets photographers review lightweight JPEGs, select at Capture level, and import the paired JPEG, RAW, or both without modifying the source. Success means a large shoot feels immediate to browse, selections are hard to lose, imports are trustworthy, and the application remains quiet enough for photographs to dominate.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, precise, trustworthy. The product should feel considered and humane, with the clarity, hierarchy, disciplined spacing, and subtle feedback associated with well-crafted Apple product interfaces. It should communicate professional confidence without performing technical complexity.

## Anti-references

- Neon or cyan-heavy “technology” palettes used as decoration.
- Dense outlines, panel borders, grid lines, glowing indicators, and sci-fi dashboard styling.
- Industrial control-room or terminal aesthetics applied to ordinary photo workflows.
- Decorative uppercase English labels, version badges, fake telemetry, and jargon that imitate professionalism.
- Nested cards, visually boxed-in layouts, and controls that compete with the photograph.
- Generic AI-generated dark dashboards with uniform spacing and excessive micro-labels.

## Design Principles

1. The photograph is the interface’s visual center; surrounding controls recede until needed.
2. Earn trust through legible states, predictable actions, and visible verification rather than technical decoration.
3. Reduce decisions during culling: navigation, keep, reject, compare, and import should remain immediately understandable.
4. Use progressive disclosure for metadata, transfer detail, and configuration so expertise does not create clutter.
5. Make the Windows desktop experience feel native, polished, and calm while preserving RawSift’s own identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Review and transfer states must never rely on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences, maintain visible focus treatment, support Simplified Chinese and English without truncation, and keep core workflows usable at the minimum 1024 × 640 window size.
